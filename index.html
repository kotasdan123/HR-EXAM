Proctor+ EXAMINATION PORTAL
SECURE ONLINE EXAMINATION

Professional examination, simplified.

Proctor+ is a browser-based examination management and proctoring portal designed to provide organizations with a structured, secure, and professional environment for online assessments.

From room-based examination access and configurable timers to security monitoring and centralized reporting, Proctor+ helps administrators manage the complete examination lifecycle from one platform.

⌁
Room-Based Access

Participants can securely enter an assigned examination using a room number and passcode.

◷
Controlled Examination

Configure examination duration, availability schedules, and proctoring controls based on operational needs.

✓
Security Monitoring

Detect common tab switching, focus loss, fullscreen exits, and restricted shortcut attempts.

▤
Centralized Reporting

Review examination sessions, completion status, violations, and room activity from the administrator dashboard.
